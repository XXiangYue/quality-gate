# Source and attribution

The project was separated from the author's integrated implementation commit
`f6818662fdf4d845732545c34b63776a3960b694`. `PROVENANCE.json` binds copied files to original hashes. Python package
names and the CLI were adapted for standalone use; historical measurements stay
bound to their original commit and protocols.




Quality Gate's new labels, independent acceptance logic and controlled missing-check variants are authored project work. Historical KB statistics in the old report are background only; this standalone evaluator does not depend on or vendor the old KB repository.

Third-party files retain their original license notices. This packaging operation
does not assign a new blanket license to the author's original portfolio code.

For browser-trajectory experiments, the pinned AgentRewardBench observation archive,
metadata and the OSU WebJudge implementation are downloaded from their respective
publishers. The [reproduction guide](docs/WEBJUDGE.md) includes exact revisions
and source links. Replay loads the upstream judge after verifying its SHA-256.
The public numeric trial export includes trajectory keys and observation hashes.
